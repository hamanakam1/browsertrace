# browsertrace brand

## Identity
- **Name:** browsertrace (one word, lowercase)
- **Pronunciation:** browse-trace
- **Meaning:** browser + trace — records and traces browser/dashboard usage
- **Tagline:** Observe. Trace. Understand.
- **Descriptor:** Local-first dashboard usage tracing extension

## Typography
- UI: system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- Mono: ui-monospace, Menlo, monospace

## Voice
- Clear, direct, privacy-first
- Never alarmist; never promotional about surveillance
- Emphasize consent, locality, and user control

## Do / Don't

**Do:**
- Say "consent is required before recording"
- Say "everything stays on your machine"
- Say "you can delete any recording"

**Don't:**
- Say "spy", "monitor without consent", "stealth", "keylogger"
- Suggest capturing arbitrary websites or full desktop by default
- Imply data leaves the device
